The Beagle Blog

The Beagle is a breed of scent hound, similar in appearance to the much larger foxhound.The beaglewas developed primarily for hunting rabbit or hare, known as beagling. Possessing a great sense of smell and tracking instincts,the beagleis primary breed used as a detection dog for prohibited agricultural imports and foodstuffs in quarantine around the world. The beagle is intelligent and is a popular pet due to its size, good temper, and a lack of inherited health problems.
